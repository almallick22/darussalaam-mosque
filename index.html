Who We Are

Darus Salaam Mosque is a place of worship, learning and community, welcoming Muslims and neighbours alike while upholding the teachings of Islam. We are committed to bringing people together and building a welcoming, inclusive community, working with faith groups, community leaders, local services and charities to create opportunities, strengthen our community and support people of all ages.

We value diversity, respect and transparency, and believe that by working together, we can build a stronger future and make a lasting difference. Join us and be part of the journey.
